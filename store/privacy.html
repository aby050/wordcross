WordCross Privacy Policy

RVA Studio · Effective 28 September 2026

In short: WordCross does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Your game progress stays on your device.

What the app stores

To remember where you left off, WordCross saves the following only on your device:

  • your current level, coins and puzzle stars;
  • play statistics (puzzles solved, play time, accuracy) and the days you played, used for your streak;
  • your settings, such as theme, sound, vibration and input style.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or choose Settings → Reset progress.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email, phone number or location collected.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties.

Children

WordCross is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Links

The app can open this page and your email app to contact support. Anything you choose to send us by email is used only to reply to you and is not shared.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the app stores before that version is released.
